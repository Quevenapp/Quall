Quall Studio for OBS adds the source Quall Studio (LAN mirror) to OBS Studio: it receives the screen or the camera of a phone, tablet or computer running Quall Studio on your network.

It installs quall-obs.plugin in ~/Library/Application Support/obs-studio/plugins/, for your user only. A newer version replaces the one already there.

Quit OBS Studio before you continue.

Uninstall: quit OBS and move quall-obs.plugin from that folder to the Trash.
